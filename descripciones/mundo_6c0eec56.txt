In the heart of Aérpolis, a colossal storm swirls over a spire of glass, its jagged lightning crackling like silver threads against a midnight sky. The scene is framed by a sweeping horizon of misty clouds tinted in deep blue and violet, while shards of golden lightning illuminate the spire’s crystalline facets, reflecting a warm, gold glow. A faint red aurora arcs across the upper horizon, casting an ethereal glow over the city’s rooftops, while a subtle green mist curls around the base of the tower, adding depth and contrast. The composition balances the dynamic energy of the storm with the calm, orderly geometry of the spire, rendered in a hyper‑realistic, almost painterly style that emphasizes light and shadow.
