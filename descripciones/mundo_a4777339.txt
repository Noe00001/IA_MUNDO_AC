Above Aérpolis, a dramatic storm gathers, its jagged lightning cracking across a sky bruised in violet and gold, illuminating the floating archipelago of verdant islands. The city’s glass spires glint in the electric glow, casting fractured reflections that mingle with the deep blue haze of the clouds below. A lone wind‑torn flag, red as a warning, billows against the storm’s roar. The composition centers on a sweeping vista, where distant islands recede into mist, while a cluster of lightning bolts frames the foreground, their arcs echoing the city’s jagged silhouette. The scene is rendered in a hyper‑realist style, with crisp shadows and a dramatic chiaroscuro that heightens the impending turbulence.
